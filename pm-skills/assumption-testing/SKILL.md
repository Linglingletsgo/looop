# assumption-testing

Name the decision, cost of being wrong, reversibility and highest-risk belief. Distinguish value, usability, feasibility and business viability; investigate only risks material to this decision.
Phrase the key assumption falsifiably and define the success/failure observation before testing. Choose the cheapest permitted method that can answer it. Past behavior is stronger than hypothetical enthusiasm; a click shows interest, not retention or willingness to pay.
Use existing evidence where sufficient. Do not prescribe interviews, paid recruitment, production experiments, multiple audits or a fixed schedule by default. Record a proposed experiment as a plan until actually executed.
Report evidence limitations, contradictory findings and whether to proceed, adjust, reject or wait. Preserve the current-state schema; missing evidence is a legitimate result. Project permissions override suggested research methods.
