# requirement-brief

Use current project context and research to state who has the problem, their current workflow, the proposed improvement, alternatives and why the change matters. State missing evidence honestly; ask a human only when a material decision truly needs their input.
Define observable success criteria, scope and non-goals. Keep the overall product outcome distinct from the next implementation slice; the solution must deliver the stated value. Do not invent target metrics, contacts or market segments to fill a template.
For an active goal keep its title, value and criteria stable; update verified progress, remaining work and next slice. Complete the goal only with evidence for all criteria. Passing checks or merging a PR alone is insufficient.
Use the caller's exact bounded state/proposal schema. Do not create a new PRD file per run, force an eight-section template, require a delivery deadline or add an Acceptance stage. Respect project-owned approval responsibilities; a PM brief is not execution permission.
